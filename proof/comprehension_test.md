# Five-person comprehension test

The question this answers: can someone outside property get the main point of the dashboard in about a minute?

## Who
Five people who don't work in real estate or finance. Friends and family are fine. Don't explain anything first.

## How
1. Open https://mithuncyriac.github.io/dubai-lead-desk/ on their phone.
2. Say only: "Have a look at this for one minute." Time it.
3. Take the phone back. Ask the questions below in order and write down their words, not your summary of them.

## Questions and what counts as a pass

| # | Question | Pass if they say something like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | In one sentence, what happened to Dubai home sales? | Fewer homes sold than usual / about a third fewer since February |
| 2 | Which homes held up best? | Cheaper ones / under a million / smaller homes |
| 3 | Did prices fall more or less than sales? | Less |
| 4 | If you were buying, what's one thing you'd do differently after seeing this? | Any sensible answer (check sold prices, take time, negotiate on bigger homes) |
| 5 | Was anything confusing? | Not scored. Write it down exactly. |

The test passes if at least 4 of 5 people get questions 1 and 2 right.
