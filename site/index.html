      The lead desk

      A small tool that reads each enquiry and checks the budget against what homes are selling for

      An enquiry arrives by form or message
      It reads area, bedrooms, budget, timing and payment
      It checks the budget against DLD's recent sale prices
      It sorts the lead into Hot, Warm or Cold
      It drafts a reply that an agent approves before it is sent

      Built in n8n, free to run. Repeat enquiries from the same phone are merged into one lead.

      How well it sorts leads, tested against answers it never saw
